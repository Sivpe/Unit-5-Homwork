Teacher Panel

Past Simple & Present Perfect Homework · Power Up Level 6, Unit 5 (pp. 56–57) · Class 6B · 50 questions · 100 points · 25 minutes. Companion homework to the class quiz — all 50 exercises are new.

Lesson Aim

To consolidate, through independent homework, students' control of the past simple and present perfect — form, the finished-time vs. still-true distinction, and since / for — following the class quiz on the same grammar.

Learning Objectives

  • Choose between past simple and present perfect using time clues (yesterday, ago, last… vs. just, yet, never, ever, already, since, for).
  • Produce correct forms: irregular past simple (drank, fell, began, won, bought, met, got, left) and have/has + past participle (seen, been, eaten, written, ridden, dropped).
  • Use since + start time and for + length of time accurately.
  • Spot common errors: did/didn't + past form, have went, present perfect with a finished time.

Desired Learner Outcomes

  • By the end of the homework, learners can score across all five parts with answers sent to the class sheet (tab: PastSimplePresentPerfectHW).
  • Learners can explain, from the review screen, why each corrected answer uses its tense ("Yesterday = finished → past simple").
  • Learners below 60 revisit the clue-word rule on p. 57 before the next lesson.

Running notes

  1. Setting it: Share the link and tell the class: "Type your full name, then tap Begin. The timer starts straight away — 25 minutes for 50 questions. Do it carefully at home, on your own."
  2. Say: "Two kinds of questions. TAP — tap one answer. WRITE — type only the verb form, not the whole sentence. Spelling must be correct; capital letters don't matter."
  3. Suggested timing: Part 1 ≈ 4 min · Part 2 ≈ 5 min · Part 3 ≈ 3 min · Part 4 ≈ 6 min · Part 5 ≈ 5 min · 2 min to check.
  4. During: The top bar shows the countdown and how many questions are answered — no live score is shown to students.
  5. Finishing: "Finish & mark" warns about unanswered questions and jumps to the first gap. At 0:00 the homework auto-submits; blanks count as wrong.
  6. After: Scores land in the sheet tab PastSimplePresentPerfectHW (parts /20, total /100, time used). Walk through the review screen together next lesson — every question shows the correct answer and a one-line reason.
  7. Next student: Use "Reset attempt" on the results screen to return to the name gate.

"Reveal answers" highlights the correct option on every TAP question and shows the key under every WRITE question — use it on the projector after marking, or before setting the homework to preview.
